Analytics

I use Microsoft Clarity to see how the site is used. It records clicks, scrolling and mouse movement on these pages, along with your device, browser, screen size and approximate location, and can replay a visit. It sees nothing outside this site, and text typed into pages is masked.

For visitors in the EU, the UK and Switzerland, Clarity sets no cookies, so each page view is recorded on its own. Elsewhere it sets cookies (such as _clck and _clsk) to link the pages of one visit. Microsoft processes this data under the Microsoft Privacy Statement.
