DialyzeAI · in production at Alta

Every customer call, reviewed

Call-center QA for Alta, a Georgian electronics retailer. Every recorded call is transcribed and scored on the fields the client defines.

  • A truth-check gate verifies each AI finding against the transcript before it reaches a report.
  • Optional checks of whether operators gave customers correct information.
  • Ships as a Windows app with embedded dashboards, locked to the client's own Google Cloud project.
  • 11 change requests in 7 months, each answered within a business day and shipped within two.

Saqme To Do · open source

A to-do app your AI can run

Markdown to-do lists you and your AI edit together, on desktop and phone.

  • Claude and other assistants connect through an MCP connector, set up in one click.
  • The engine is written twice, PowerShell and JavaScript, kept identical by one shared test suite.

  • Optional sync in your own Cloudflare account; lists can be shared by single-use link.

Weld seam analysis for engineering simulations

A desktop tool that parses, analyzes and visualizes ANSYS simulation data. I was the only engineer for six months.

  • Finds weld seams by part or material name and draws their interface nodes and coordinate systems on the mesh.
  • Plugins for fatigue cycles and a materials library, plus load-case groups and points of interest.
  • 3D renderer written from scratch in OpenGL; about 26,000 lines of C++.
  • Shipped with a user manual and four developer manuals, validated with the supervising engineer.
