What makes it work unattended

  • Two publishing slots a day, driven by a scheduler. The only human step is one approve-or-redo message in Telegram.
  • A separate model fact-checks every script against its sources before anything is voiced.
  • Automatic QA looks at rendered frames and re-records the voice when it's off.
  • Every source was checked by hand against its robots.txt before being added.
  • Each Sunday the system takes its three best and three worst reels of the last 30 days and rewrites its own prompt inputs, so measured retention shapes the next script.

DialyzeAI

Satli product, in production at Alta

Stack

Client
Alta, a Georgian electronics retailer
Product page

Call-center QA: every recorded call transcribed and scored on the fields the client defines.

1–3% → 100%of calls reviewed

Alta's quality team could listen to 1–3% of its calls. Now every call is transcribed, scored and shown in dashboards. 400,000 minutes is about 6,700 hours, three and a half years of one person listening.

Built for a real customer

  • A truth-check gate verifies each AI finding against the transcript before it reaches a report.
  • Optional checks of whether operators gave customers correct information.
  • Credentials are locked to the client's own Google Cloud project, with billing, config migration and an updater built in.
  • Ships as a Windows app with embedded dashboards.
  • Every one of Alta's 11 change requests was answered within a business day and shipped within two. Two of them I delivered before Alta asked.

Saqme To Do

Open source, 2026

Stack
JavaScript, PowerShell, Cloudflare Workers, MCP
Try it Code

Markdown to-do lists that you and your AI can both edit, on desktop and phone.

  • Claude and other assistants connect through an MCP connector, with one-click setup.
  • The task engine is written twice, PowerShell on Windows and JavaScript in the browser and on the phone. One shared test suite keeps them behaving identically.
  • AI can edit your lists but has a delete budget, so a model can't wipe them.
  • Optional sync runs in your own Cloudflare account. Lists can be shared by single-use link, read-only or read/write.
  • Tests run in CI on every push.

Saïna Mechanical Analysis Tool

Internship at Saïna, France, 2024–2025. Sole engineer, six months

Stack
C++, OpenGL, GLSL, Dear ImGui, Python, CMake

A desktop application that parses, analyzes and visualizes ANSYS simulation data, focused on weld seam analysis.

  • Parses ANSYS results and load cases, with a Python parser for load-case files.
  • Weld seams: found automatically by part or material name, with their interface nodes, numbering and coordinate systems drawn on the mesh, and editable thickness, type and material.
  • Plugins for fatigue cycles and a materials library, plus load-case groups and points of interest.
  • Selection by node, element, part or sub-assembly, and saved projects.
  • 3D renderer written from scratch in OpenGL, about 26,000 lines of C++ in total.
  • Documented with a user manual and four developer manuals, validated with the supervising engineer.
